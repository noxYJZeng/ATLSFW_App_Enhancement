About ATLSFW

ATLSFW App is a digital platform dedicated to uniting Atlanta’s sustainable fashion ecosystem. We make it easier for users to explore eco-conscious brands, attend impactful events, and stay informed on the latest trends and news.

The fashion industry produces 10% of global carbon emissions. In Atlanta, sustainable fashion is fragmented and inaccessible. The ATLSFW App brings together events, news, and ethical vendors into a single platform to empower eco-conscious choices.

Key Features

Upcoming Events

Discover a curated list of upcoming sustainable fashion events in Atlanta, including fashion shows, panel discussions, pop-up shops, and more. Each event listing provides detailed information such as time, location, organizers, and descriptions, along with a one-click RSVP function to mark your interest.

Workshops & Repair Cafés

Participate in community-driven hands-on experiences such as clothing repair cafés, textile reuse tutorials, and sustainable material workshops. Events are filterable by type and include dedicated styling and visual cues that differentiate them from other listings.

Featured Brands

Browse a selection of vetted sustainable fashion brands operating locally or nationally. Each brand profile includes a description of their eco-friendly practices, product highlights, and a direct link to their website or store within the app.

Ticket Information

Secure your spot at high-demand events through an integrated ticketing section. Listings provide clear calls-to-action, direct external links to ticket platforms, and real-time updates on availability, pricing tiers, and deadlines.

Personalized Home Page

Each user's home screen is dynamically tailored with content such as saved events, relevant articles, and recommended brands. Built-in pull-to-refresh, contextual loading indicators, and error messages ensure a responsive and intuitive experience.

Admin Event Management

Admin users have access to a full suite of content moderation tools, including editing and deleting events, confirming vendors, managing user-generated content, and handling tickets or approvals directly from their admin dashboard.

App Demo

The ATLSFW App supports three distinct types of users—regular users, vendors, and admins—each with tailored features and responsibilities. Users can browse sustainable fashion events, save favorite articles, and personalize their dashboard. Vendors can manage their brand presence, update shop information, and interact with community content. Admins oversee the entire ecosystem, from approving vendors and moderating posts to organizing events and maintaining platform quality.
